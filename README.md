# PROLOGUE — The Reading Club of LBSITW

> **Where Stories Begin, Ideas Grow, and Voices Find Their Pages.**

**PROLOGUE Membership Portal** is a responsive web portal created for **PROLOGUE – The Reading Club of LBS Institute of Technology for Women (LBSITW)**.

The main purpose of this website is to provide students with a centralized platform where they can learn about PROLOGUE, explore its activities, discover books, view events and gallery content, and most importantly, **register for PROLOGUE membership**.

## About the Project

The **PROLOGUE Membership Portal** is designed to provide students with an easy and engaging way to discover and become part of the reading club.

Through the portal, students can:

- Learn about PROLOGUE
- Understand the club's vision and purpose
- Explore the benefits of joining
- Discover books and reading recommendations
- Explore literary activities
- View upcoming and past events
- Explore the PROLOGUE gallery
- Register for PROLOGUE membership
- Access the PROLOGUE member sheet after registration

### Membership Journey

```text
Visit PROLOGUE
      ↓
Learn About the Club
      ↓
Explore Activities & Books
      ↓
View Events & Gallery
      ↓
Register for Membership
```

# Key Features

## Home
Introduces PROLOGUE and provides navigation to the main sections of the portal.

## About PROLOGUE
Provides information about the club, including its purpose, vision, mission, and literary activities.

## Why Join PROLOGUE
Highlights the benefits of membership, including reading, creative writing, communication, discussions, and participation in literary activities.

## Membership Registration
Allows students to register for PROLOGUE membership through an online form with client-side validation.

### Registration Details
- Full Name
- Email Address
- Phone Number
- College / Department
- Year of Study

## Member Sheet
Provides access to the PROLOGUE member sheet after registration.

## Books & Reading
Offers an interactive book collection with categories such as Classics, Fantasy, Science Fiction, Poetry, Mystery, Indian Literature, and Malayalam.

Includes mood-based book discovery and reading recommendations.

## Ask PROLOGUE AI
Provides an interactive feature for exploring books and receiving reading recommendations.

## Activities
Showcases PROLOGUE activities such as:

- Reading Circles
- Creative Writing Workshops
- Open Mic & Poetry
- Literary Quizzes
- Debates & Discussions
- Book Discussions
- Literary Film Screenings

## Events
Displays upcoming and past PROLOGUE events with relevant event details.

## Gallery
Provides a categorized gallery with filtering, captions, lightbox viewing, and navigation features.

## Contact
Provides a contact form with validation for name, email, and message.
