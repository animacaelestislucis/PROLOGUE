About PROLOGUE

The reading club of LBS Institute of Technology for Women — built for readers, writers, thinkers, and dreamers.

Our Story

PROLOGUE began with a simple belief: that stories connect us, ideas spark change, and every voice deserves a page. We are a community of students who love to read, write, and explore the world through literature.

“Books are the plane, and the train, and the road. They are the destination, and the journey. They are home.”

by Anna Quindlen

Through regular reading circles, creative writing workshops, open mics, debates, and performances, we create space for expression, growth, and connection. PROLOGUE is not just a club — it's a chapter in every member's story.

A Space for Every Voice

Whether you're a voracious reader, a shy poet, or a passionate debater - PROLOGUE welcomes you exactly as you are.

Vision · Purpose · Mission

Vision

To cultivate a vibrant literary culture within LBSITW where every student feels empowered to read, write, and express.

Purpose

To provide a welcoming space for literary exploration, creative expression, and meaningful discussion.

Mission

Through reading sessions, writing workshops, performances, and discussions, we nurture confidence, critical thinking, and a lifelong love for literature.
